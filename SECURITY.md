# Security policy

This is the default policy for every Navree repository that does not have its own. The framework's policy, with the table of supported versions, is in [NavreeHQ/navree](https://github.com/NavreeHQ/navree/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@navree.com**. Never open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a suspected vulnerability, and do not post details anywhere public before a fix is released.

You can also use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting: open the repository's Security tab and choose "Report a vulnerability".

Please include:

- the repository, crate or package, and its version;
- what an attacker can do, and under what conditions;
- a minimal reproduction or proof of concept;
- whether you plan to disclose publicly, and when.

## What to expect

| Step | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement of your report | within 2 business days |
| Fix for a critical issue | within 24 hours of confirmation |
| Fix for a high issue | within 72 hours of confirmation |
| Fix for medium and low issues | in the next regular release |

We tell you the severity and our reasoning, keep you updated, and credit you in the advisory unless you ask us not to.
